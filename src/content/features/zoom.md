# Zoom

`zoom` scales the content of a slide without changing the canvas.

```md
---
zoom: 0.8
---
```

In the headmatter it applies to all slides. In a slide's frontmatter it applies to that slide, and wins over the headmatter. Use it to fit a slide that is a little too full. For scaling part of a slide, see [Transform](./transform.md).
